About Lakewood Estate

Lakewood Estate is a multi-phase residential community. The residents' committee manages upkeep, finances, events and inter-phase coordination — this site brings the paperwork online so it's easier for everyone to find and use.

Committee members can post announcements, update banners and manage forms directly through the admin area, without needing to touch any code.
